PDF-only draft. Scientific check: the paper claims 9 unbound candidates with P_ub > 0.5; this is supported by Sec. 4.1, Figure 2 caption, Table 1, and Table 2. Table 2 lists exactly 9 Gaia DR3 rows, all with P_ub values greater than 0.5. The last two Table 2 rows are noted as previously reported by Marchetti (2021) and Li et al. (2021); the others are treated here as introduced by this paper. Solar parameters are method context, not structured gold: Sec. 2 final paragraph adopts (x_sun, y_sun, z_sun) = (8.122, 0, 0.0208) kpc and (U_sun, V_sun, W_sun) = (11.1, 245, 7.25) km s^-1. Potential/orbit context is also unscored method context: Sec. 3 adopts Gala MilkyWayPotential for the Milky Way, including Hernquist bulge and nucleus, Miyamoto-Nagai stellar disk, and NFW dark matter halo; the LMC is modeled with a Plummer profile of mass 1.38e11 Msun, half-mass radius 5 kpc, and scale radius 17 kpc, with LMC phase-space parameters from Erkal et al. (2019b), MW dynamical friction, and galpy MovingObjectPotential/ChandrasekharDynamicalFrictionForce for backward orbit integrations. Table 2 also reports P_disk and v_phi, but the current gold vocabulary does not score disk-origin probability or rotational velocity, so they are left in notes rather than structured quantities.

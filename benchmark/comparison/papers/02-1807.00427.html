Expert notesfinal_annotation 
PDF check: the paper identifies 16 HiVel candidates from Gaia DR1/TGAS plus LAMOST/LSS-GAC DR4, but Sec 4.3 explicitly marks only HiVel14, HiVel15, and HiVel16 as unbound; this draft records those three unbound cases. LSS-GAC DR4 is the LAMOST Galactic Anti-Center catalog/release used for line-of-sight velocities and stellar parameters, so LAMOST DR4 vs LSS-GAC DR4 is not a conflict in this paper. Sec 2.2: distances are Astraatmadja & Bailer-Jones (2016) Bayesian TGAS parallax distances; Galactic coordinates l,b and distance are transformed to Galactocentric X,Y,Z with R_sun=8.2 kpc. Sec 2.2: velocities use V_LSR=220 km/s and solar peculiar motion (10.0, 11.0, 7.0) km/s. Sec 4.3: orbital/boundness discussion uses the McMillan (2017) Galactic potential. Table 2 prints l,b but no RA/Dec, so observed_phase_space.ra/dec are left absent; this is valid because RA/Dec quantities are optional and should not be hand-converted from l,b.
AI summaryliterature current extraction 
3 unbound high-velocity stars (HiVel14, HiVel15, HiVel16) from a sample of 16 HiVel stars identified from 436 kinematically-selected local halo stars from Gaia DR1 (TGAS) + LAMOST DR4. The paper explicitly states: '3 HiVel stars (HiVel14, HiVel15 and HiVel16) are unbound due to their very high velocity.' These are FGK-type stars with V_gsr = 644, 753, 734 km/s respectively. The remaining 13 HiVel stars have bound orbits. The paper focuses on halo kinematics and chemistry, not primarily HVS discovery. Gaia DR1 (TGAS) source IDs are provided in Table 2. No Gaia DR2 data used.
